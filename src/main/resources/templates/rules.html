Game Rules

Flexidle - a guessing game where you have to guess the correct word with color-clues.

The length of each row is corresponding to wordlength, and the amount of rows shows how many guesses you have.


But what about the colors-clues, then?

A gray color on your guessed letter means that it is not a part of the correct word:


Yellow, however, means that the letter you guessed is in the word but in the wrong spot:


When your guessed letter turns green you have made a correct placement of that letter:


When your guessed the right word - everything is green:
